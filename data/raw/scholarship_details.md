# Scholarship Details

## Government Scholarships

The college is registered on the National Scholarship Portal. Eligible students can apply
for Post Matric Scholarships for Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, and Other Backward
Class students. The Central Sector Scheme of Scholarships covers 82,000 fresh scholarships
every year for meritorious students. Students must apply on the National Scholarship
Portal with their Aadhaar, bank account details, and income certificate before the
deadline announced each academic year.

## Institutional Scholarships

The merit scholarship covers full tuition fees for the top student of each branch every
semester. Students with a CGPA above 9.0 and 100 percent attendance receive a Rs. 10,000
cash award each semester. The merit-cum-means scholarship covers 50 percent of the tuition
fee for students with family income below Rs. 2.5 lakh per year and a CGPA above 8.0.
Sports scholarships of Rs. 15,000 per year are given to students representing the college
at state or national level competitions.

## Need-Based Financial Aid

Students facing financial hardship can apply for the student emergency fund through the
Student Welfare Office. The fund provides one-time assistance up to Rs. 25,000 for medical
emergencies, hostel dues, or academic fees. Applications are reviewed by the welfare
committee within two weeks and require a recommendation letter from the class mentor.

## Application Process

Scholarship applications open every year in August. Students fill the online application
form, upload supporting documents such as marksheets, income certificates, category
certificates, and bank passbook details. The scholarship committee verifies documents and
releases the beneficiary list. Disbursement happens directly to the student bank account
through the Direct Benefit Transfer system.

## Renewal Rules

Scholarships are renewed every year based on academic performance. Students must maintain
a minimum CGPA of 6.5 and clear all backlogs to continue receiving scholarship benefits.
Scholarship benefits are discontinued if a student fails two consecutive semesters or
is found to have submitted false information in the application.
