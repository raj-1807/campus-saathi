# Events and Notices

## Tech Fest

The annual technical festival TechNova is held in the second week of March. The fest
includes coding competitions, robotics, hackathons, paper presentations, and workshops.
Registration for events opens two weeks before the fest through the events portal.
Students from all branches can participate and winning teams receive cash prizes and
certificates.

## Cultural Fest

The cultural fest Samvedna takes place in the first week of February. Events include
dance, music, drama, fashion show, and literary competitions. Inter-college teams can
participate with prior registration. The cultural committee announces the event schedule
and audition dates through notices on the student portal and notice boards.

## Sports Meet

The annual sports meet is held in January at the campus sports complex. Events include
athletics, cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, and table tennis.
Students register through their class sports representatives. The top performers are
selected for the university level sports meet.

## Workshops and Seminars

Departmental workshops on topics such as machine learning, cloud computing, web
development, and data science are organized throughout the semester. Industry experts
conduct guest lectures every month. Workshop schedules are posted on department notice
boards and the events portal. Participation certificates are provided to attendees.

## Important Notices

Exam time tables are published at least two weeks before examinations. Internal assessment
schedules are announced in the first week of every semester. Fee payment deadlines and
scholarship application windows are notified on the portal. Students must regularly check
the student portal notices section and their registered email for official
communication.
