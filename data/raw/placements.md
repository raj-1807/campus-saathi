# Training and Placement Knowledge

The training and placement cell manages company registrations, student eligibility lists,
resume collection, aptitude rounds, technical interviews, HR interviews, and offer letters.

Demo placement statistics: 150 plus companies visited campus, highest package is 45 LPA,
average package is 8.5 LPA, and 92 percent of eligible students were placed.

Students should prepare resumes, coding profiles, aptitude practice, project explanations,
and company-specific interview questions before placement drives.
