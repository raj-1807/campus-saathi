# Library Timings

## Working Hours

The central library is open from 8 AM to 10 PM on all working days. On Saturdays the
library is open from 9 AM to 5 PM. The library remains closed on Sundays and public
holidays. The reading hall is open 24 hours during the examination period with the
issue counter open from 8 AM to 8 PM.

## Services and Sections

The library has separate sections for text books, reference books, journals, and
digital resources. Book issue counter operates from 9 AM to 6 PM. Reference books,
journals, and magazines are for in-library reading only and cannot be issued.
The digital library section provides 40 computer systems with e-journals, e-books,
and online databases such as IEEE, Springer, and Science Direct.

## Membership and Issuing Rules

All registered students get library membership with their college ID card. A student
can issue up to 4 books at a time for 15 days. Books can be renewed online for another
15 days if no one else has reserved them. A fine of Rs. 5 per day is charged for books
returned after the due date. Lost books must be replaced with a new copy or the current
market price plus fine must be paid.

## Library Card and ID

The library card is printed on the college ID card at the time of admission. A duplicate
ID card with library access costs Rs. 200 and is issued by the library office. Students
leaving the campus must clear all library dues and return all issued books to get their
no-dues certificate.

## Librarian Support

The librarian support desk helps students find books, use the digital catalogue, and
place book reservations. Book purchase requests can be submitted online and are reviewed
by the library committee every month. Reference help is available from 9 AM to 6 PM
on working days.
