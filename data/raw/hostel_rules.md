# Hostel Rules

## Admission and Allotment

Hostel admission is separate from academic admission. Students apply through the hostel
portal and rooms are allotted on a first-come first-served basis. Boys and girls hostels
are separate blocks with their own wardens and security. Students must complete hostel
registration, pay the hostel fee, and collect their room keys from the warden office.
Change of room requires written permission from the hostel warden.

## Timings and Entry Rules

The hostel main gate closes at 10 PM on working days and at 11 PM on weekends. Students
must sign the entry and exit register at the security desk. Latecomers must provide a
valid reason and are reported to the warden. Visitors are allowed in the common room
from 4 PM to 7 PM and must deposit their ID cards at the security desk. No visitors are
allowed in student rooms.

## Room Conduct

Students are responsible for the furniture and fittings in their rooms. Damages are
charged to the student account. Electrical appliances such as heaters, induction cookers,
and kettles are not allowed in rooms. Cooking inside rooms is strictly prohibited.
Students must keep rooms clean and maintain silence after 10 PM.

## Mess Rules

The mess serves breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner. Mess timings are breakfast
7:30 to 9 AM, lunch 12:30 to 2 PM, snacks 5 to 6 PM, and dinner 8 to 9:30 PM. Mess
menus are announced weekly. Students must register for meals on the mess app to reduce
food wastage. Special food requests during illness must be reported to the mess manager.

## Leave and Discipline

Students going home must inform the warden and fill the leave register. An overnight
leave requires parental consent through the hostel portal. Absence without permission
for more than three days is reported to the parents. Ragging, smoking, alcohol, and
drugs are strictly prohibited and are punishable with expulsion from the hostel and
college. Repeated rule violations lead to a disciplinary committee review.
