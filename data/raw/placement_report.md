# Placement Report

## Placement Statistics

The training and placement cell reports strong placement results every year. More than
150 companies visited the campus in the last academic year. The highest package offered
was 45 LPA by an international tech company. The average package was 8.5 LPA and the
median package was 7 LPA. 92 percent of eligible students were placed before the end of
the final semester.

## Recruiting Companies

Top recruiters include technology companies, product companies, banking and finance
firms, and consulting organizations. Core engineering companies hire from civil,
mechanical, and electrical branches. Information technology companies hire the largest
number of students from computer science and information technology branches.
The placement cell maintains a database of more than 300 companies.

## Placement Process

The placement process includes company registration, student eligibility list
preparation, resume collection, aptitude rounds, technical interviews, HR interviews,
and offer letters. Companies announce job descriptions and eligibility criteria before
the drive. Students apply through the placement portal for each drive. Shortlisted
students attend the written test and interviews on campus.

## Preparation Support

The placement cell conducts aptitude training, mock interviews, group discussion
practice, and resume building workshops from the sixth semester. Students get access
to coding practice platforms and mock tests. Company-specific preparation material is
shared before each drive. Students should prepare their resumes, coding profiles,
project explanations, and company-specific interview questions before placement drives.

## Eligibility for Placement

Students in the final year with no active backlogs are eligible for placement drives.
Some companies require a minimum CGPA of 6.0 to 7.5 and specific skill sets. Students
with backlogs are allowed to register after clearing them. Internship offers are made
to second and third year students through the internship cell.
