# Student Services Knowledge

The library is open on working days from 9 AM to 8 PM. Services include book issue,
renewal, digital resources, reading rooms, and librarian support.

Student services include fee payment, scholarships, hostel management, campus transport,
ID card renewal, library services, and helpdesk support.

For complaints, students should select a category, describe the issue, add location details,
attach proof if available, and track the status from submitted to assigned, under review,
resolved, or escalated.
