# Fee Structure

## Tuition Fees

The tuition fee for B.Tech programs is Rs. 95,000 per year. The tuition fee for M.Tech
programs is Rs. 80,000 per year. MBA program tuition fee is Rs. 1,20,000 per year.
Fees can be paid online through the student portal using UPI, net banking, debit card,
or credit card. An offline payment counter is available in the administrative block on
working days from 10 AM to 4 PM.

## Other Charges

A one-time admission fee of Rs. 5,000 is charged at the time of admission. The university
registration fee is Rs. 3,000 per year. Caution money of Rs. 10,000 is refundable after
completion of the program. The library and laboratory fee is Rs. 4,500 per year.
The students welfare fund contribution is Rs. 500 per year.

## Hostel Fees

Hostel fees for double sharing rooms are Rs. 60,000 per year and include lodging,
mess charges, laundry, and Wi-Fi. Single room hostel fees are Rs. 85,000 per year.
Hostel admission is separate from academic admission and must be applied for through
the hostel portal. Hostel fees are non-refundable after the second week of the semester.

## Scholarships and Waivers

Students belonging to reserved categories are eligible for government scholarship schemes
through the National Scholarship Portal. Students scoring above 90 percent in the entrance
exam receive a 25 percent tuition fee waiver. Merit-cum-means scholarships cover 50 percent
of tuition fees for students with family income below Rs. 2.5 lakh per year.

## Late Fee and Refund Policy

A late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies if fees are not paid by the last date. If a student
withdraws before the start of classes, the full tuition fee minus Rs. 5,000 is refunded.
Withdrawal in the first two weeks of the semester gives a 50 percent refund. No refund is
given for withdrawals after the first month of the semester.
