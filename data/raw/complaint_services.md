# Complaint Registration Process

## How to Register a Complaint

Students can register complaints through the college portal under the complaints section,
or through the student services mobile app. Walk-in complaints are accepted at the student
helpdesk counter in the administrative block from 10 AM to 5 PM on working days. Every
complaint gets a unique complaint ID that is used to track the status.

## Complaint Categories

Complaints are grouped into categories: academics, hostel, mess and food, fees and
finance, infrastructure, library, transport, security, harassment, and others. Students
must select the correct category so the complaint reaches the right department. Misrouted
complaints are forwarded by the helpdesk within one working day.

## What Information to Provide

For a good complaint, students should select a category, describe the issue clearly with
dates and times, add location details like building and room number, and attach proof
such as photos or receipts if available. Contact details are used by the concerned
department to update the student about the resolution.

## Status Tracking

The complaint status moves through these stages: submitted, assigned, under review,
resolved, or escalated. Students receive updates through the portal and email at every
stage change. If a complaint is not resolved within 7 working days, it is automatically
escalated to the registrar office.

## Harassment and Safety Complaints

Complaints related to harassment, ragging, or safety are handled by the internal
complaints committee with confidentiality. Students can register these complaints
anonymously. The committee takes up these complaints on priority and resolves them
within 15 working days. Students facing an emergency should contact the campus security
control room which operates 24 hours a day.
