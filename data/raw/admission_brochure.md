# Admission Brochure

B.Tech admission is completed through online registration, entrance score submission,
document upload, counselling, seat allotment, fee payment, and physical verification.

## Eligibility Criteria

Admission to B.Tech programs requires passing Class XII (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry,
and Mathematics as compulsory subjects and a minimum aggregate of 60 percent marks.
Admission is based on the entrance exam rank and the counselling process. Diploma holders
can take lateral entry into the second year of B.Tech programs based on their diploma
marks and seat availability.

## Required Documents

Required documents include Class X marksheet, Class XII marksheet, entrance scorecard,
migration certificate, character certificate, category certificate if applicable,
Aadhaar card, and passport-size photographs. Original documents are verified at the time
of physical document verification after seat allotment. Two sets of photocopies are
required for the admission file.

## Counselling Process

After the entrance results, students register for counselling and fill their choice of
branches in order of preference. Seats are allotted based on rank and category quota.
Students must confirm the allotted seat within the deadline by paying the seat acceptance
fee. If a student misses a counselling round, they can participate in the next round as
long as seats are available.

## Key Dates

Application forms open in the first week of April. The application deadline is the end of
May. Entrance results are announced in June, followed by counselling rounds in July.
Classes for the academic session begin in the first week of August. Fee payment must be
completed before the start of the session.

## Quota and Reservation

Seats are reserved as per government norms: 15 percent for Scheduled Caste, 7.5 percent
for Scheduled Tribe, 27 percent for Other Backward Classes, and 10 percent for Economically
Weaker Sections. Category certificates issued by the competent authority must be uploaded
during registration and verified during document verification.
